1. INTRODUCTION

Student Needs Portal is a web-based application developed to help students easily find essential services such as hostels, rental rooms, mess facilities, and private libraries. The system acts as a centralized platform where students can search, compare, and access verified information about these services.

Many students relocate to new cities for education and face difficulties finding reliable accommodation, food facilities, and study environments. This project aims to solve that problem by providing a single digital platform that connects students with service providers and simplifies the search process.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Students often depend on social media groups, local contacts, and offline inquiries to find accommodation and other facilities. This creates several problems:

  • Lack of verified information.
  • Time-consuming manual searching.
  • Difficulty comparing available options.
  • No centralized platform for multiple student services.
  • Limited access to reviews and feedback.

3. OBJECTIVES OF THE PROJECT

  • To develop a centralized student service portal.
  • To provide hostel and room rental listings.
  • To display mess services with pricing and details.
  • To provide information about private libraries.
  • To allow students to search and filter services.
  • To maintain verified listings through admin approval.
  • To improve transparency through ratings and reviews.
  • To reduce the effort required to find essential facilities.

4. SCOPE OF THE PROJECT

The project focuses on providing students with easy access to accommodation, mess services, and private libraries through a web application.

The system supports three types of users:

Student

Search services, compare facilities, submit ratings and reviews, contact providers.

Service Provider

Manage hostel, mess, and library listings, view student inquiries, track view counts.

Administrator

Verify providers, approve/reject listings, moderate student reviews, monitor platform metrics.

5. MAIN FUNCTIONAL MODULES

  • Authentication Module: Student and provider registration and login.
  • Accommodation Module: Hostel, PG, and rental room listings with details and pricing.
  • Mess Services Module: Display of mess facilities, menus, timings, and charges.
  • Private Library Module: Library search, membership information, and operating hours.
  • Search & Filter Module: Search services based on location, budget, and preferences.
  • Feedback Module: Ratings and reviews for all listed services.
  • Admin Module: Verification of providers, management of listings, and moderation of reviews.

7. KEY ADVANTAGES & CONCLUSION

  • Centralized platform for comprehensive student needs.
  • Verified and reliable service information through admin gatekeeping.
  • Easy search and multi-facet filtering options.
  • Time-saving, zero-brokerage, and convenient.
  • Improved transparency through verified student reviews.
  • Better accessibility for students relocating to new college cities.
Project Outcome: A fully functional website developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and MySQL that provides students with quick access to verified services and improves their overall experience while relocating for education.
